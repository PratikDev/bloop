# Art paragraph: what's actually built

First written as a read-only check of `bloop-dev` on 9 Nov 2026. **Updated 10 Nov 2026 by the ART build** (`wt/art`,
docs/statement/ART.md): every row's new status. All new features are behind the `art…` flags in `src/lib/flags.ts`
(`ready` false until the integrated full pass); on in dev and preview, or with `NEXT_PUBLIC_ART_*=1`.

| # | Item | Status (9 Nov) | Status now (10 Nov) | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Science ↔ Art dial (0 / 0.5 / 1) + test that data pitch is identical at every position | NOT BUILT | **BUILT** (flag `artDial`) | Settings and every Official record: three stops, Measure / Blend / Music (`src/components/Art/Dial`), with "Data voice: unchanged at every position. What changes: …" generated from the stop's layers (`src/lib/art/dial.ts`). Layers: gridline ticks, bed, chord through the hook (`src/lib/art/chord-hook.ts`, CV's layer at integration), drone on Sa, reverb, swara + meend (`src/lib/art/meend.ts`, ported from I3). The check renders live SST, IMERG rain and SVS 3912 offline at art off, 0, 0.5, 1 (and 1 in a monsoon month) and measures the data voice's settled pitch from the audio (`src/lib/art/proof`, e2e/art/dial.e2e.ts). |
| 2 | Raw-pixel demo (naive pixel→pitch vs Bloop, measured flatness/roughness) | NOT BUILT | **BUILT** (flag `artRawPixel`) | How we know › "Pixel to sound, or Bloop?": SVS 3912's first frame, the naive hue scan and Bloop's rule, 6 s each, with stored flatness, entropy and roughness (`public/data/art/raw_pixel.json`, `scripts/art-raw-pixel.ts`, `src/lib/art/raw-pixel`). |
| 3 | Style skins (e.g. Bengali ensemble) validated against mapping constraints | NOT BUILT | **PARTIAL**: Earth Orchestra built; Bengali ensemble skin planned | Earth Orchestra (flag `artOrchestra`): one synthesised instrument family per Earth system, the Instrument style setting, the category earcon, the Official grid grouped by system, the legend card (`src/lib/audio/orchestra`). The Bengali ensemble skin is a Tier 3 plan (`docs/statement/ART_ROADMAP.md` §6). |
| 4 | "What is data / what is artistic choice" panel per record | PARTIAL | **BUILT** (flag `artChoices`) | "From the data:" and "Our artistic choice:" for every rule in About this sound and How we know, and every Official record's channels, generated from the rule, the category and the dial (`src/lib/art/choices.ts`). |
| 5 | Proof-in-unison beats | BUILT | BUILT (unchanged) | as before. |
| 6 | Swara + meend / scale-quantised mode | NOT BUILT (app) | **BUILT** (dial at Music) | The data voice starts on the nearest Megh note and glides exponentially, landing on the data pitch after 0.12 s (only where notes last ≥ 0.25 s, so a fast sweep still settles). TS test: 200 values within 1 cent (`src/lib/art/art.test.ts`). The "1 step = measured error" legend is not built (see ART.md). |
| 7 | Story suites / guided tours with dataset named per step | PARTIAL | **BUILT** (flag `artChoices` for the tour line, `artJourney` for the suite) | The tour names the dataset and period on every step (`src/components/Art/StoryDataset.tsx`) and ends with a letter to the future; a second suite, Water's journey (lightning → river → rain → GRACE → Bay of Bengal), with its own player and a deterministic WAV (`src/components/Art/Journey`). |
| 8 | "What changed?" A/B of two dates at one place | PARTIAL (code done, off) | **BUILT** (already on: `FLAGS.expedition` ready since 9 Nov, bloop-dev `8576669`) | Nothing changed by ART. |
| 9 | Exploration tools: multiple probes, transect, lens/zoom | PARTIAL | PARTIAL (plans only) | Probes and transect are a Tier 3 plan (`ART_ROADMAP.md` §1). |
| 10 | Emotion questions in /listen-test | NOT BUILT | **BUILT** (flag `artListenTest`) | Two 5-point ratings (valence, connection) after a sound-on and a sound-off clip of the same data, counterbalanced; `testVersion` 2 in the export; summary and protocol updated. |
| 11 | Ear-vs-eye task in /listen-test | NOT BUILT | **BUILT** (flag `artListenTest`) | "When did the rain peak?" once heard, once seen (two halves of the storm), counterbalanced; answers from the data. |
| 12 | Synced visual playhead during time sequences | BUILT | BUILT, and extended | Your lifetime's charts and Water's journey's step list follow the audio clock. |

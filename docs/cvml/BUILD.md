## A. The panel and the error budget

- **Box statistics in the browser** (`src/lib/cv/box-stats.ts`): n cells, % no data, P10/P50/P90 (the engine's own
  `percentile`, now exported from `mapping-maths.ts`), a 16-bin histogram over the record's own axis. Boxes: the
  needle's neighbourhood (the describe facts' 30° span) and the five named boxes, defined once in
  `src/lib/cv/boxes.json` (the panel and the tests read the same file).
- **Records:** live SST 5101, IMERG latest and the 48-frame sequence (the frame PageUp/PageDown hold), and every
  official record's played layer at the step on screen (3912: all 66 steps). Pattern-only records show no numbers
  ("pattern only: no values read"); relative records show no units.
- **The test** `pipeline/tests/test_box_stats.py` (the gate's `c1_boxstats.py` promoted): numpy and the browser
  function (`scripts/cv/box-stats.ts`) agree on all 580 box-frames (counts and histograms exactly, percentiles to 1e-6).
- **Cents budget:** `pipeline/stats/error_budget.py` → `public/data/meta/error_budget.json`, from the mapping files
  (semitonesPerUnit's maths) and each record's measured error (VALIDATION_TABLE.md or the data file). Flagged (> 100 ¢):
  Sound terrain zoom (computed live from today's span), SST anomaly 5176, GRACE Bangladesh, EIC 5148, EIC 5151,
  5425 ocean2 temperature, 4950 forest height. GISTEMP and NDVI: "error not known", never 0.
- **Panel:** a heading with its toggle on Listen and on official record views; box choice; record, frame, box, median,
  P10–P90, % no data, n cells, validation status, the budget line ("Our measured error is about 0.31 °C: about 19 cents
  of pitch"; on flagged axes "Pitch differences under about N semitones here are within our error"); How we got this; a
  braille-safe short line ("SST med 29.8C P10-90 29.1-30.3 err 0.31C"); the every-axis budget table.

## B. The value chord

- `src/lib/audio/layers/chord` implements the shared `ChordLayer` exactly and exports `valueChord`
  (`ValueChordLayer extends ChordLayer` adds `setView`, `modes`, `subscribe`).
- **Groups (B1, `modes.ts`):** histogram at σ/10 bins, Gaussian smoothing with σ = the record's measured error (from
  error_budget.json: 0.31 °C live SST, 0.644 °C 3912), at most 2 peaks with prominence ≥ 5 % of the top peak and
  ≥ 5 % of the box's cells, split at the valley. Unit tests: unimodal → 1, bimodal → 2, closer than σ → merge, ≤ 2,
  empty or unknown σ → none.
- **Sound (B2):** each group a sustained soft pad (sine + weak 2nd and 3rd harmonics, 250 ms attack) at
  `mapVoice(record's rule, value)`; gains ∝ area fraction, the total 8 dB under the melody; rolled low → high 80 ms
  apart; hysteresis (changes only when a group appears or disappears, or moves more than σ). Off in Both and the
  proof-in-unison duet ("Chord off: … two data voices"); official play options with two voices count as Both.
- **Honesty gate (B3):** verified / stated → chord; relative → chord, "relative values", no units; pattern only → none,
  with the reason; colour bars: `pipeline/stats/chord_allowlist.py` → `public/data/meta/chord_allowlist.json` from each
  bar's CIELAB lightness profile (sequential / diverging → full; the 3912 bar; jet-like dark low end → modes only;
  others → none).
- **Text (B4):** toggle "Chord: spread of values" in the panel and Settings (off for first-time visitors, remembered);
  the status region says each change once ("Two groups: 18.9 °C (40 %) and 20.4 °C (60 %)"; brief: "Chord: 2 groups";
  short lines: "Chord 2 grp 18.9C 40% 20.4C 60%"); How we got this.
- **T1** (`chord.test.ts`): tones within 1 cent of the record's rule; gains ∝ shares; ≤ 2 voices; the gate for every
  status and mode; either owner keeps it on; `stop()` releases both; hysteresis. 11/11 with the modes tests.
- **T2** (`pipeline/tests/test_colormap_invariance.py`, the gate's c6 promoted; MUR from the 3912 truth files):

  | Box | Variant | Median spread | Mode spread | Modes | Hue L1, bar vs viridis |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Bay of Bengal (2007-11-18) | clean | 0.017 °C | 0.01 °C | 1/1/1 | 2.0 |
  | Gulf Stream (2008-02-13) | clean | 0.063 °C | 0.009, 0.03 °C | 2/2/2 | 1.466 |
  | Bay of Bengal | JPEG q75 (reported) | 0.047 °C (P10 0.023) | 0.04 °C | 1/1/1 | |
  | Gulf Stream | JPEG q75 (reported) | 0.025 °C (**P10 0.754 °C**) | 0.0, 0.05 °C | 2/2/2 | |

  All clean spreads are under σ = 0.31 °C; the hue control differs clearly against viridis. Outputs:
  `public/data/meta/colormap_invariance.json`, `public/images/cvml/colormap_invariance_*.png`.
- **T3** (a real 3912 frame vs MUR, same date): not done; it needs the 22-minute 3912 re-fetch (known limit).

## E. Listener test

Two tasks, each its own module in `src/lib/listen-test/chord/`, registered in the one list in `tasks.ts`: variety
(2AFC, today's widest vs narrowest P10–P90 box; the answer is the wider) and groups (today's one-group or two-group box,
drawn once per session, played as the chord; the answer is the chord's own count). Same consent, logging and export;
`docs/LISTEN_TEST_PROTOCOL.md` and the summary script updated; `testVersion` untouched (ART's).

## Known limits

- **No inversion confidence shipped.** The per-pixel residual exists in the pipeline but no shipped grid stores it;
  the panel leaves it out and says so.
- **T2 is synthetic:** renders of NASA MUR data; real frames add compression, anti-aliasing and line effects. T3 not run.
- **Jet's low tail:** under JPEG the Gulf Stream P10 moved 0.754 °C (> σ); modes and medians held. Jet-like bars get
  the chord with modes only.
- **Flagged cents axes:** the seven above; pitch steps under their error carry no meaning, and the app says so.
- The chord is off for official play options with two voices (it would otherwise replace the second voice).
- Log axes (PM2.5, NOx) get a budget in decades but no chord (σ has no value units there).

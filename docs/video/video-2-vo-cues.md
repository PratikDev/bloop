## Putting your voice on the film

Export your voiceover as one WAV that starts at 0:00 of the film (silence where there's no line), then:

```sh
ffmpeg -i bloop-video-2.mp4 -i voiceover.wav -filter_complex "[0:a][1:a]amix=inputs=2:normalize=0,alimiter=limit=0.95[a]" -map 0:v -map "[a]" -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 256k -movflags +faststart bloop-video-2-final.mp4
```

Or drop both files into any editor (CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, Premiere) with the voice on its own track.

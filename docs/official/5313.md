## How

- Assets by rule (`docs/official/5313_assets.md`): 5313's own frames are a 7680×2160 EIC cut with zooms, so its alternate versions are used: 5276's lights-only change still and annual frames (lights on black, no base map to unmix) and the change colorbar, and 5475's 2048×1024 Science On a Sphere frame for the georeference.
- Georeference: the 5475 frame's land base map (pixels away from its own ocean colour) against Natural Earth 1:50m land: IoU 0.928 (passes at 0.9; shifted bounds score lower in every case). The 5276 still is the same world stretched to 16:9: its brightness peaks against the 5475 lights at shift (0, 0) (r 0.743).
- Colorbar: Labels are words ('lights out', 'no change', 'new lights'), no numbers: read as relative change, −1 (lights out) … +1 (new lights), linear between the strip ends. The white point (no change) sits at 0.497 of the strip.
- Change: chroma-mode inversion (matcher `rgb-nearest`, max distance 148.4) of pixels with brightness ≥ 0.1 (linear); 6.5% of lit pixels decode. Per 0.1° cell, the brightness-weighted mean change.
- Annual brightness: the brightest channel of each annual frame (2012, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023), averaged per cell. These frames have no colorbar.

## Known limits

- Relative values only: the colorbar's labels are words, so the change is −1 … +1 with no unit, and the annual brightness is the picture's, not radiance.
- The change still is a JPEG: chroma subsampling mixes each small light with its dark neighbours, which pulls colours toward white ('no change'). Direction survives; magnitude is biased toward 0, so only 23,770 cells reach |change| ≥ 0.5.
- Most lit pixels are too dim to read a colour from: the change is known only where lights are bright enough.
- Validation tiles were chosen from the frame alone (h18v04, h17v03, h09v05, h29v05, h22v05, h29v06, h26v06, h27v06); VNP46A4 is the product the visualization was made from, so the check tests the decoding, not Black Marble.
- Stored at 0.1° to stay under the 25 MB budget.

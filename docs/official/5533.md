## What it is (SVS text)

NASA utilizes satellite instruments and models to monitor sources of air pollutants and their movement through the atmosphere. This visualization shows concentrations of air pollutants, such as Particulate Matter (PM2.5, fine particles smaller than 2.5 micrometers), Ozone (O~3~), Carbon Monoxide (CO), and Nitrogen Oxides (NO~x~) as they are tracked from NASA's Goddard Earth Observing System Composition Forecasting (GEOS-CF) system.

## How

- Assets by rule (`docs/official/5533_assets.md`): 5533 has no flat 2:1 frame set, so the 2048×1024 species layers of its Science On a Sphere version 5518 are used (Carbon monoxide (CO), Nitrogen oxides (NOx), Ozone (O3), PM2.5), each with its own colorbar; the banded composite is not used.
- Frames: 1801 per species. One frame in 3 was fetched whole; for the others only the PNG prefix holding the rows down to the Bangladesh box was fetched and decoded (bit-identical to a full decode). Raw frames deleted after decoding.
- Frame times: linear: 1 Jul 2024 00:00 – 14 Sep 2024 00:00 UTC ÷ 1801 frames (hourly); no date is burned into any 5518/5533 frame. The GEOS-CF v1 hourly axis has exactly one step per frame over the same span, which supports the mapping.
- Georeference: equirectangular assumed; coastline outlines found as thin dark lines on all four species, ocean flood-filled from fixed seeds, IoU against Natural Earth 1:50m land 0.952, 0.901 (passes at 0.9; 1-px outline growth gives 0.914, 0.920); the same bounds shifted by ±0.5° / ±1° scored lower in every case.
- Colorbars (OCR, labels and scale): Carbon monoxide (CO): 40, 100, 130, 200, 500 (piecewise); Nitrogen oxides (NOx): 10^-3, 10^-2, 10^-1, 10^0, 10^1 (piecewise); Ozone (O3): 5, 20, 40, 60 (linear); PM2.5: 0.4, 1.6, 6.4, 25.6, 102.4, 409.6 (log).
- Colour model per species, chosen by rule on a frame's own colours: Carbon monoxide (CO): exact (95.8% of pixels on the colorbar); Nitrogen oxides (NOx): opacity (78.8% of pixels on the colorbar); Ozone (O3): opacity (38.2% of pixels on the colorbar); PM2.5: exact (95.9% of pixels on the colorbar).
- Opacity ramps (O3, NOx): learned as a monotone curve from colours whose value is identifiable, then frozen; below the curve's lowest opacity a pixel is 'below range': Nitrogen oxides (NOx): opaque from 0.00198, below range under 0.00109 ppbv; Ozone (O3): opaque from 32.5, below range under 19.5 ppbv.
- Outputs: a global daily layer per species (1.0° cells, 2-day means of the 3-hourly frames) and the Bangladesh box (lat 20–27, lon 88–93) at native resolution for every hourly frame.

## Known limits

- GEOS-CF is a model: these are model values, not measurements.
- Frame times are approximate (linear over the stated period); the validation pairs each frame with the hourly mean that starts at its time and reports ±1 h as sensitivity.
- The SVS page links the GEOS-CF v2 datashare, but no public v2 archive covers the period (the v2 replay store starts 2025-08-04); the same-model check uses v1.
- O3 and NOx below their colorbars' visible range are censored, not decoded: decoded share 74.5% (O3) and 88.3% (NOx) of pixels; below range 25.4% and 11.3%.
- The CO and NOx colorbars are not uniform scales (CO: evenly spaced ticks labelled 40, 100, 130, 200, 500; NOx: labels not on the tick marks), so values between labels are interpolated (linear for CO, log for NOx).
- Ground comparison: of the 27 PM2.5 sensors within 25 km of Dhaka, 1 had data in the period: insufficient ground data, not a correlation result (team decision, 29 Oct 2026).
- PM2.5 ships as relative values (team decision, 29 Oct 2026): of the 11 PM2.5-type GEOS-CF v1 variables and sums tried, none matches the frames at ~1× with median error ≤ 15 %. The field that tracks the frames best is `chm_inst pm25_rh35_gcc (surface)` (Spearman 0.997), at frame ≈ 0.313 × the model (median error 69.7%); the ratio nearest 1 is `chm_tavg pm25su_rh35_gcc` (1.754×, Spearman 0.840). Pattern and timing are verified; the colorbar is not rescaled.
- NOx ships as values with a stated error: median error 16.7% against GEOS-CF v1 misses the provisional 15 %; accepted explicitly by the team on 29 Oct 2026.

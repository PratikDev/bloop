This is the 'beauty shot version' of Perpetual Ocean 2: Western Boundary Currents.  The visualization starts with a rotating globe showing ocean currents. ## Known limits

- Which water (report correction 3, added 31 Oct): the page says 'the flows from the surface down to 600 meters deep are all white' and only deeper flows are coloured. On SVS 5479's flat maps of the same run, the decoded temperature sits closest to NOAA WOA23 at 1000 m (surface: ρ 0.68, Δ -8.02; 600 m: ρ 0.69, Δ -2.20; 1000 m: ρ 0.72, Δ -1.11); salinity at 600 m (surface: ρ 0.53, Δ +0.03; 600 m: ρ 0.64, Δ +0.10; 1000 m: ρ 0.55, Δ +0.05). The surface checks (a) are kept for the record but compare the wrong depth. The temperature and salinity layers carry no white flows (0 % white); the white flows are the beauty version's. 5479 lists no colorbars, and only 8.8% of its temperature flow pixels and 1.0% of salinity lie on 5425's bars, so the bars are not simply reused.
- A different ocean model at depth (added 3 Nov, `svs/records/ocean2/glorys.py`): GLORYS12 monthly means on the same 5479 frames: temperature 600 m ρ 0.69 / Δ -2.19, 1000 m ρ 0.73 / Δ -1.07; salinity 600 m ρ 0.61 / Δ +0.10, 1000 m ρ 0.53 / Δ +0.05. Not run: the ECCO2 same-model check (no reachable 2021–2023 mirror). Period conflict kept: the text says 2021–2023; SVS 5505's dataset entry says 13 Nov 2022 – 25 Dec 2023; the dates layer and 5479's burned-in labels run 2021–2023.
- No map position (perspective camera views): regional comparisons only; flow speed cannot be scaled to m/s.
- SVS served ≈ 20 KB/s on 29 Oct 2026, so two frames per view were decoded; map layers exist for Kuroshio ['2022-01-25 (frame 6650)'], Agulhas ['2022-08-17 (frame 10250)'], Gulf Stream ['2023-02-02 (frame 13250)', '2023-06-24 (frame 15750)'], World (5479) ['2022-11-12 (frame 11800)', '2023-02-01 (frame 13240)', '2023-04-24 (frame 14680)', '2023-07-14 (frame 16120)', '2023-10-04 (frame 17560)', '2023-12-25 (frame 19000)']; the other frames (decoded before their grids were kept) give the regional numbers only.

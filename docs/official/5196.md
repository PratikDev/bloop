## How

- Assets (`docs/official/5196_assets.md`, `5436_assets.md`): 5196's frames are globe views; its Science On a Sphere version 5436 has the CO2 volume alone as 8192×4096 EXR with alpha, the Earth surface alone, a per-frame date file and the colorbar. 12 volume frames of 1177, evenly spaced, were streamed and deleted.
- Colorbar: labels 380, 400, 420, 440, 460 ppm; colour and opacity separated from the checkerboard drawn under the strip (P = α·C + (1 − α)·B with the two checker backgrounds). The colour is fully opaque from 424.9 ppm.
- Values: each pixel un-premultiplied, normalised by its brightest channel (the render's lighting scales brightness) and matched in chroma mode. A volume render averages a column, so a value is the opacity-weighted ppm along the view.
- Georeference: the surface layer's land against Natural Earth: IoU 0.760 (lower at every shift; winter snow and sea ice are white like land).
- Plumes: pixels at or above 424.9 ppm; area and edge length per frame (km, cos-latitude).

## Known limits

- Relative values from a model nature run; not measurements.
- Not invertible: 380–408, 421–427 ppm (the colorbar's white end, and a fold). Cells in the white end are stored as ≤ 408 ppm.
- Opacity below about 420 ppm is read from a checkerboard with cell-to-cell shading, so it is uncertain; it weights the column, not the matched colour.

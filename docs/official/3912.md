## What it is (SVS text)

This visualization shows sea surface current flows. The flows are colored by corresponding sea surface temperature data. This visualization is rendered for display on very high resolution devices like hyperwalls or for print media.This visualization was produced using model output from the joint MIT/JPL project entitled Estimating the Circulation and Climate of the Ocean, Phase II (ECCO2). ECCO2 uses the MIT general circulation model (MITgcm) to synthesize satellite and in-situ data of the global ocean and sea-ice at resolutions that begin to resolve ocean eddies and other narrow current systems, which transport heat and carbon in the oceans. The ECCO2 model simulates ocean flows at all depths, but only surface flows are used in this visualization. ||

## How

- Assets by rule (`docs/official/3912_assets.md`): the 2048×1024 `orig` frame set, the SST colorbar image and SVS's per-frame date list (method a). Every 6th frame was fetched (334 frames, one per 24.77 h); raw frames deleted after decoding.
- Georeference: equirectangular −180…180 / −90…90 assumed and checked against Natural Earth 1:50m land: IoU 0.938, 0.937 (passes at 0.9); the same bounds shifted by ±0.5° / ±1° scored lower in every case.
- Colorbar: OCR read the labels 0, 10, 20, 32; scale linear, 8.72 px per °C; the SVS caption's stated range 0–32 °C is the cross-check (strip ends decode to 0.15 °C to 31.91 °C).
- SST: the streaks are the colorbar's colours with the grey floor removed and dimmed, so values come from hue (mode `hue`, matcher `rgb-nearest`, max distance 10.0, minimum chroma 0.1). Between streaks stays empty: median 48.2% of ocean pixels lie on streaks; a 1° cell needs 20% of its pixels on streaks.
- Motion: OpenCV Farnebäck between consecutive daily frames (pattern drift: how the drawn eddies and meanders move, not the water) and the structure-tensor streak axis (the current's axis, direction modulo 180°).

## Known limits

- ECCO2 is a model: SST differences against MUR include real model–observation differences.
- The pattern drift's 99th percentile is 14.5 km/day (0.167 m/s), while OSCAR's median in the strong-current cells is 0.47 m/s: the drift is the pattern's speed, not the water's (motion along a long uniform streak is invisible to optical flow). The click voice says so.
- Direction from the streaks is an axis; its sign comes from the pattern drift, which follows the current only where features move downstream.
- Stored at 1.0° and 4-day means to stay under the 25 MB budget.

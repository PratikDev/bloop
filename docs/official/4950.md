## How

- Assets (`docs/official/4950_assets.md`): the four 3840×2160 print TIFs of the global view with its burned-in colorbar.
- Colorbar, read from each frame: ticks 0, 20, 30, 50; scale linear, 16.29 px per metre. OCR read ['0', '51', '20', '30', '46', '50']; ['51', '46'] disagree with the line through the other 4 labels and are dropped as misreads. 2 label(s) not on a tick mark (0, 50): label-centre position, ±5 px.
- Height: chroma-mode inversion (the globe is lit, so brightness follows the light) of land pixels (not blue ocean, not black space, not the colorbar), max distance 12.0; 11.8% of globe land pixels decode.
- No georeference: the stills are 3D globe views with exaggerated height. Layers stay on the picture's own grid.

## Known limits

- Pattern only: the decoded heights do not match GEDI L3 (median 31.2 m vs 5.7 m, KS 0.73): the lit render decodes mostly where tall columns face the light, so the decoded histogram is biased toward tall canopy.
- No map position: the needle moves across the picture, not across the Earth.

## How

- Assets (`docs/official/4950_assets.md`): the four 3840×2160 print TIFs of the global view with its burned-in colorbar.
- Colorbar, read from each frame: ticks 0, 20, 30, 50; scale linear, 16.29 px per metre. OCR read ['0', '51', '20', '30', '46', '50']; ['51', '46'] disagree with the line through the other 4 labels and are dropped as misreads. 2 label(s) not on a tick mark (0, 50): label-centre position, ±5 px. Of the page's three colorbar files (0–40, 0–50, 0–75 m), the stills carry the 0–50 m bar: their own scale spans 54.0 m, as the 0–50 m file does (0–40 m spans 43 m, 0–75 m spans 81 m).
- Height (layer): chroma-mode inversion of land pixels (the globe is lit, so brightness follows the light), max distance 12.0; 11.8% of globe land pixels decode.
- Height (validation, added 30 Oct): flat, unshaded pixels only — colours within RGB distance 10.0 of the colorbar itself, i.e. no lighting change (4.7%–8.8% of land pixels).
- Georeference for validation: each still's globe is fitted as a perspective view (circle, then sub-camera point, roll and camera distance by Natural Earth land IoU in the inner disc): IoU 0.63, 0.79, 0.95, 0.91.
- Check: GEDI L2B RH100 footprints (GEDI02_B v002, 22 granules, 5,539,411 quality shots, May 2019 – Apr 2020; the source SVS names: dataset 'Relative Height', 'derived from the L2B LIDAR metric RH100'), projected into each still and gridded with the flat pixels into 48-px cells.

## Known limits

- Pattern only: over all four stills the flat heights do not meet the check (Spearman 0.10, median difference +9.4 m, 105 cells). Per still: IoU 0.63: ρ -0.14, +27.4 m (32 cells); IoU 0.79: ρ -0.09, +13.3 m (25 cells); IoU 0.95: ρ 0.67, +0.9 m (18 cells); IoU 0.91: ρ 0.51, +2.9 m (30 cells). Where the globe fit is good (IoU ≥ 0.9) heights agree within a few metres; where it is poor they do not — the georeference of a 3D view, not the colour decode, limits this check (an observation, not a pass).
- Before (superseded): all decoded pixels vs GEDI L3 over all land, no location: median 31.2 m vs 5.7 m, KS 0.73.
- No map position for the layer: the needle moves across the picture, not across the Earth.

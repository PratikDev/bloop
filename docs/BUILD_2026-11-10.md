# Build of 10–11 Nov 2026: CV/ML, art and musicality, every shipped dataset

Three specs were built in parallel, each on its own branch in its own worktree, from bloop-dev `8576669`, then merged
into bloop-dev in the order CV → DATA → ART and wired together:

- **CV/ML** (the CV/ML spec): what the jukebox read, the cents error budget, the value
  chord, the colour-bar proof card, the mix pass and Check 5, two chord tasks in the listener test.
- **ART** (the art, emotion and musicality spec): Earth Orchestra, the Science ↔ Art dial and its
  pitch-identity proof, raw pixel vs Bloop, the letter to the future, your lifetime and MIDI, data vs artistic choice,
  the tour naming every dataset, the listener test's emotion and ear-vs-eye tasks, Water's journey, the season drone.
  Tier 3 is plans only (`docs/statement/ART_ROADMAP.md`).
- **DATA** (the data-maximize spec): Data health, the leftovers, fire weather + Fire Geiger +
  the Fires record, landslide exposure, night lights, NASA's radar method, Ask Bloop's data questions, the raw-download
  triage.

Per-spec detail: `docs/cvml/BUILD.md`, `docs/statement/ART.md`, `docs/data-integration-status.md`,
`docs/official/REJECTED.md`, `docs/audio/MIX.md`.

## Honest wording (Video 2)

Say:
- "The data voice never changes; the dial only changes the instruments around it."
- "The chord is the spread of values Bloop read from NASA's colour bar; it sounds the same whatever colours were used
  (tested on synthetic renders of NASA MUR)."
- "Earth Orchestra: each Earth system has its own instrument family."
- "A letter to the future, written only with measured values" (for categories: "a forecast class, not a measurement").
- "Fire weather is an experimental one-day forecast; a FIRMS hotspot is a pixel with one or more fires."
- "Landslide exposure is modelled, not observed. A cloudy night is a gap, not darkness."
- "NASA's version: where things are. Bloop's version: what the values are."

Never say: "AI hears the Earth", "colour becomes music", "real-time computer vision", "emotional AI", "the Earth's real
voice", "proves sound reveals what eyes miss" (unless the listener test shows it, with n), "music that represents
climate grief", "predicts fires", "blackout", "landslides happened".
